Institutional Overview

20 Years of Girls' Islamic Education in Quetta

Founded in 2006, the Institute of Islamic Education (For Girls) is a girls madrasa in Quetta created with a clear vision: to establish an academic haven where young women receive classical Islamic education alongside modern analytical skillsets.

Over two decades, we have developed a secure, supportive environment instructed entirely by highly qualified female scholars. We emphasize intellectual rigor, spiritual development, and practical community leadership.

Core Institutional Commitments

  • ✓ Strictly Female Faculty: Managed and taught by experienced female Islamic scholars.
  • ✓ Safe & Protected Environment: Dedicated campus infrastructure equipped with top security.
  • ✓ Recognized Curriculum: Authenticated theological content paired with contemporary practical training.
